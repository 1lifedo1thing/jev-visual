What the model sees

One full-size 640×480 screenshot, exactly the game you see. The model selects the numbered column containing the white ball. The paddle moves smoothly toward that fixed lane center, just like pressing a Lane button. No coordinates, velocity, predicted landing point or computed direction are supplied. A larger ball, wider paddle and clean background are shared by human and AI play. A target remains active for up to 800 ms, then stops until a fresh decision arrives. Responses older than 800 ms are not executed.

This is an experiment, not a proven game-playing agent. The model can miss the ball or choose poor moves. Candidate probabilities are normalized scores, not accuracy estimates. AI selects one of five visible lanes. The actuator reads only the paddle position and selected lane, never the ball position. Results and test details.
